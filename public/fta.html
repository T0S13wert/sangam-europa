The EU–India trade agreement

Talks began in 2007, stalled in 2013 and resumed in June 2022. The text was concluded in January 2026; it has not yet been signed.

Where it stands

  1. DoneNegotiations concluded

    27 January 2026, at the 16th EU–India Summit in New Delhi.

  2. DoneProposal to the Council

    11 September 2026: the Commission proposes signature and conclusion.

  3. ReportedSignature

    Reported for 16 December 2026; not yet confirmed by the Council.

  4. ExpectedEuropean Parliament consent

    Expected one to two months after signature.

  5. ExpectedEntry into force

    Expected in early 2027, after India’s approval.

European Commission, The Hindu, European Parliament

    Duties EU exporters are expected to save, according to the European Commission.

Outside the agreement

  1. Investment protection

    Negotiated separately since June 2022; not concluded.

    European Parliament

  2. Geographical indications

    A separate agreement, also still under negotiation.

    European Parliament

  3. Carbon border adjustment (CBAM)

    No exemption. India receives an assurance that any flexibility the EU grants another country will also apply to India.

    Reuters

  4. Steel

    Duty-free quotas, with volumes reported differently by the two sides; above quota, the EU’s 50% tariff applies.

    Reuters

  5. Cross-border data flows

    Deferred to a review within five years.

    Indian Express

  6. Movement of people

    Short-term mobility of professionals is covered in the services chapter. Wider labour mobility runs through a separate EU–India memorandum signed the same day.

    European Commission
