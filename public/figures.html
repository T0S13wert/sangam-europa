Selected figures on trade, investment, population and energy. Each chart gives its source, and the full data can be downloaded as a spreadsheet.

How much each side matters to the other

For the EU, India is the ninth-largest partner in goods. For India, the EU is among the three largest. Investment runs mostly one way: the stock of EU direct investment in India is almost ten times the reverse.

What each sends the other

Goods trade by product group in 2025. Machinery travels in both directions in almost equal value; chemicals, textiles and metals flow mainly from India to Europe, transport equipment and instruments mainly from Europe to India. Totals: €48.9 bn from the EU to India, €69.5 bn from India to the EU.

Emissions per person, and clean power

India emits more carbon dioxide in total than the EU, but less than half as much per person. Low-carbon sources produced 27% of India’s electricity in 2025 and 71% of the EU’s.
