Timeline, 1791–2026

Key dates in relations between India and Germany, and between India and Europe. Each entry is marked as culture and scholarship, or as politics and trade.

Culture and scholarshipPolitics and trade
  1. 1791
    Culture and scholarship

    Georg Forster’s German Śakuntalā, translated from William Jones’s English version. Goethe’s verses on the play appear the same year.

    J.-U. Hartmann, LMU

  2. 1808
    Culture and scholarship

    Friedrich Schlegel publishes Über die Sprache und Weisheit der Indier in Heidelberg.

    Deutsches Textarchiv

  3. 1816
    Culture and scholarship

    Franz Bopp’s study of the Sanskrit verb system founds comparative Indo-European linguistics.

    Britannica

  4. 1818
    Culture and scholarship

    August Wilhelm Schlegel is appointed at the new University of Bonn, which becomes Germany’s first centre of Indology.

    Who Was Who in Indology

  5. 1823
    Culture and scholarship

    A. W. Schlegel prints the Bhagavad Gītā with a Latin translation. Alexander von Humboldt buys two Tamil palm-leaf manuscripts in Paris, the first in Berlin’s collection.

    Who Was Who in Indology, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin

  6. 1825
    Culture and scholarship

    Wilhelm von Humboldt reads the first of two papers on the Bhagavad Gītā to the Prussian Academy of Sciences (30 June 1825; the second follows on 15 June 1826).

    Herring, ICPR

  7. 1827
    Culture and scholarship

    Hegel replies to Humboldt in a two-part review in the Jahrbücher für wissenschaftliche Kritik.

    Herring, ICPR

  8. 1849
    Culture and scholarship

    Max Müller’s edition of the Rig Veda begins to appear; six volumes follow by 1874, much of the printing paid for by the East India Company.

    Gifford Lectures archive

  9. 1851
    Culture and scholarship

    Schopenhauer calls the Upanishads, which he read in a Latin translation of a Persian one, “the solace of my life”.

    Urs App

  10. 1896
    Culture and scholarship

    Swami Vivekananda visits the philosopher Paul Deussen in Kiel and travels on with him to London.

    vivekananda.net

  11. 1907
    Politics and trade

    Bhikaiji Cama raises an early Indian flag at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart.

    Britannica

  12. 1922
    Culture and scholarship

    Hermann Hesse publishes Siddhartha; he never travelled to India itself.

    Goethe-Institut

  13. 1930
    Culture and scholarship

    Rabindranath Tagore shows his paintings in Berlin and visits Einstein at Caputh.

    Käthe-Kollwitz-Museum

  14. 1951
    Politics and trade

    The Federal Republic and India agree to exchange diplomatic missions (7 March).

    Auswärtiges Amt

  15. 1962
    Politics and trade

    India establishes relations with the European Economic Community.

    Indian Ministry of External Affairs

  16. 1972
    Politics and trade

    India and the German Democratic Republic establish relations (8 October).

    Auswärtiges Amt

  17. 1988
    Culture and scholarship

    Günter Grass publishes Zunge zeigen, written after a year in Calcutta.

    Outlook India

  18. 1994
    Culture and scholarship

    The Tagore Centre opens in Berlin.

    ICCR

  19. 2000
    Politics and trade

    India and Germany sign the agenda for their Strategic Partnership (May). The first EU–India Summit meets in Lisbon (June).

    Embassy of India, Berlin, Indian Ministry of External Affairs

  20. 2004
    Politics and trade

    The EU and India become strategic partners.

    Indian Ministry of External Affairs

  21. 2007
    Politics and trade

    Negotiations on a broad trade and investment agreement begin.

    European Parliament

  22. 2013
    Politics and trade

    The trade talks stall after fifteen rounds.

    European Parliament, European Commission

  23. 2022
    Politics and trade

    The trade talks are relaunched (17 June). India and Germany sign a migration and mobility agreement (5 December).

    European Parliament, Federal Ministry of the Interior

  24. 2023
    Politics and trade

    The first EU–India Trade and Technology Council meets (May). IMEC is agreed at the G20 in New Delhi (September).

    Indian Ministry of External Affairs

  25. 2024
    Politics and trade

    Germany publishes its “Focus on India” strategy; the 7th intergovernmental consultations meet in New Delhi (October).

    Indian Ministry of External Affairs

  26. 2025
    Politics and trade

    The College of Commissioners visits India for the first time (February). The Commission and the High Representative propose “A New Strategic EU–India Agenda” (September).

    European Commission

  27. 2026
    Politics and trade

    Chancellor Merz in India (January). The EU–India Summit concludes the trade negotiations, adopts “Towards 2030” and signs a security and defence partnership (27 January).

    Council of the EU

  28. 2026
    Politics and trade

    The third Trade and Technology Council meets in Brussels; formal talks on Horizon Europe begin (July). The Commission proposes signature of the trade agreement (September).

    European Commission, Science|Business
