India and Europe, read in both directions.

Sangam Europa is a quarterly magazine on relations between India and Europe, edited in Berlin. The first issue appears in November 2026.

About the magazine

Why now

Read more

US tariffs, Chinese export controls and closed shipping routes pushed the EU and India together in 2026. On 27 January they concluded a trade agreement and signed a defence partnership. Most of it is still to be implemented.

Ties between people have grown faster than the institutions: more than a million residence permits, the largest share of EU Blue Cards, and rising goodwill in Germany. The magazine follows both.

European Commission, CIDOB, Eurostat, Eurostat, via The Local, Pew Research Center

Key facts

More figures

  1. 7 March 1951

    Germany and India agreed to exchange diplomatic missions. 2026 marks 75 years of relations.

    Auswärtiges Amt

  2. Winter 2024/25

    India was the largest country of origin for international students in Germany: 58,833.

    DAAD/DZHW

  3. 2025

    About 37% of the active pharmaceutical ingredients used in the EU came from India, more than from any other country.

    IW Köln for Pro Generika, via Ärzteblatt

  4. End of 2025

    Indian citizens held 1,033,926 valid residence permits in the EU, up from 755,288 at the end of 2022.

    Eurostat

  5. 27 January 2026

    The EU and India concluded their free trade negotiations, after talks that began in 2007. Signature is reported for 16 December 2026.

    European Commission, The Hindu
