People-to-People

Students, professionals and families who live between India and Europe, and the institutions that support them.

Background

  1. 2024

    Indian citizens received 16,300 of about 78,100 EU Blue Cards, more than any other nationality. Germany issued 13,300 of them.

    Eurostat, via The Local

  2. End of 2024

    Full-time employees with Indian citizenship had the highest median monthly pay of any nationality in Germany: €5,393 gross.

    IW Köln

  3. Winter semester 2024/25

    India was the largest country of origin for international students in Germany: 58,833 students, 14.6% of the total, ahead of China.

    DAAD/DZHW

  4. 2025

    Indians filed about 1.15 million Schengen visa applications, the third-largest number of any nationality.

    European Commission data

  5. 31 December 2025

    Indian citizens held 1,033,926 valid residence permits in the EU, up from 755,288 at the end of 2022. In 2025 the EU issued them 227,577 first permits, the most for work.

    Eurostat

  6. 31 December 2025

    311,130 Indian citizens lived in Germany, two and a half times as many as in 2018 (124,095).

    Destatis

  7. 27 January 2026

    The EU and India signed a memorandum on a comprehensive framework for mobility; member states take part by choice. The first European Legal Gateway Office opened in New Delhi on 18 February 2026, starting with IT professionals.

    Indian Ministry of External Affairs, EEAS

  8. Spring 2026

    63% of Germans held a favourable view of India, up from 47% in 2023.

    Pew Research Center
